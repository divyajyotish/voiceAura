Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

VoiceAuras ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our voice analysis service and how we handle it.

1. What We Collect

When you use the voice scan feature, your microphone audio is processed in real time directly in your browser to calculate acoustic metrics such as pitch, volume, and pause patterns. We do not store, upload, or transmit your raw audio recording to any server.

2. Payment Information

If you choose to unlock the full report, payment is processed securely through Razorpay. We do not collect or store your card, UPI, or bank details - these are handled entirely by Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant systems.

3. How We Use Information

Generated report data (your scores and insights) exists only in your browser session to display your report and generate your downloadable PDF. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand overall site usage (e.g. number of visits). This data is aggregated and not used to identify individual users.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information processed through our service. Payment transactions are secured via Razorpay's encrypted infrastructure.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not store your voice recordings or personal report data on our servers, there is no persistent personal data for us to retain beyond your session.

7. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, please reach out via the contact details provided on our website.
