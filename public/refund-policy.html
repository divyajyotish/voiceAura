Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

1. Digital Product

VoiceAuras's full report unlock is a digital service delivered instantly upon successful payment. Since the report is generated and made available immediately, refund requests are evaluated on a case-by-case basis as described below.

2. Eligible Refund Cases

  • Payment was deducted from your account/bank but the report was not unlocked due to a technical or payment verification failure.
  • You were charged more than once for the same report unlock due to a system error.

3. Non-Eligible Cases

Refunds are not provided simply because you are dissatisfied with the entertainment-style "Fun Insights" content, as this is clearly disclosed as non-clinical, self-reflection content before and within the report.

4. How to Request a Refund

If you believe you are eligible for a refund, please contact us with your payment ID (visible in your bank/UPI app or Razorpay payment confirmation) and a description of the issue, within 7 days of the transaction.

5. Failed/Pending Transactions

If your bank account was debited but the transaction shows as "failed" on our end, this is typically a bank or UPI network-side delay. As per standard banking practice, such amounts are usually auto-reversed to your original payment method within 5-7 working days. If this does not happen, please contact us with your transaction details.

6. Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method via Razorpay and may take 5-7 business days to reflect, depending on your bank.

7. Contact

For refund requests or payment issues, please reach out via the contact details provided on our website.
